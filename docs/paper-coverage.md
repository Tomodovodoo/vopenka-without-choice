Two clauses remain unmatched

`thm:Woodin-forcing`, paper line 648: the sharp Σ₃ bound for the specified sparse presentation.

V13 fixes the normalized sparse presentation before the theorem (lines 629–646). The proved [woodinEndpointStagePresentation_sigmaThree_uniform](../ZFVP/ModelTheory/WoodinEndpointInternalPresentation.lean#L10) instead defines `woodinStageCarrier` and `woodinLocalOrderOn`: stage-tagged conditions with the order pulled back from the raw endpoint. The [sparse source](../ZFVP/ModelTheory/WoodinSparseSourceCode.lean#L9) is built from `woodinSparseStageCode`, using an additional recoding construction. The sparse formulas have proved definability and rank agreement, but I did not find a Σ₃ complexity theorem with the required readout for that exact carrier/order. Even a proved forcing equivalence does not by itself transfer the complexity of a definition.

The construction, endpoint ZFC, rank comparisons, directed quotient bounds, relative generic movement and restricted critical lift have checked proofs. This finding is about the literal presentation-specific complexity clause. To claim it, supply a Σ₃ formula and correctness theorem for the specified sparse presentation, or justify a manuscript presentation change with the required identifications. The earlier completion node explicitly excluded the sharp bound from its contract; that exclusion cannot settle this broader audit.

`lem:Woodin-endpoint-forcing`, paper line 983: the full endpoint and generic quantifiers.

The paper asserts the uniform forcing definition at any marked strongly inaccessible endpoint computing the actual prefixes, and its truth lemma for every generic over `(V_gamma, Def(V_gamma))`. The checked [uniform coded readout](../ZFVP/ModelTheory/WoodinSparseCodedForcingEquality.lean#L140) assumes a Woodin-supercompact endpoint. The [marked-stage truth theorem](../ZFVP/ModelTheory/WoodinSparseEndpointTruth.lean#L111) takes a generic induced by an ambient `V`-generic. The adjacent theorem proves that such an induced filter meets rank-definable dense classes; its implication goes in that direction. It does not replace the ambient-generic assumption by the weaker rank-class-generic assumption.

The library contains general class-forcing truth machinery. I did not find its specialization proving these two stronger clauses for the specified Woodin presentation. The missing match would take only markedness, inaccessibility, actual-prefix agreement and rank-class genericity, and prove the fixed-level forcing readout/truth statement. An arbitrary rank-class generic must not silently be assumed to extend to an ambient generic. The manuscript gives an informal argument for this stronger scope; this audit does not refute that argument.

These are formal scope/correspondence findings, not mathematical counterexamples and not failures of the checked main-result proofs. In particular, the finite-window and finite-restoration routes have separate ambient-generic exports, so the broad auxiliary lemma's unchecked scope is not automatically a missing dependency of the consistency theorem.

Correspondence details

- A02: `omegaOneCohen_model_zfVP_DC_notChoice` has no external `Countable V`. Its generic is supplied as part of the extension construction. Countability in the consistency proof is used to obtain a model and generic from consistency, not assumed of an arbitrary ground.
- R18 / ordinary reals: `solovay_realRegularity` and `consistent_realSolovay_of_consistent_zfVP` quantify over internal Dedekind reals. Their target includes ordinary Lebesgue measurability using the full Carathéodory criterion, Baire property and perfect set property. The measurable ground data are the section's stated setup and are constructed in the consistency corollary. No unproved bridge premise remains in these exports.
- Feferman–Levy: the checked sentence uses `P(omega)` as the real carrier. This is the standard real coding for this set-theoretic countability assertion; the whole countable sequence is internal. I have not counted it as an ordinary-Dedekind-real export. No topological or measure identification is being inferred from coding.
- Finite bounds: the finite-window entry uses `woodinSparse_cn_source_positive_window` with the paper's five-variable source formula and `woodinSourceWindowLevel`, not merely the separate, larger coded-formula bound. The reflection entry uses `finite_reflection_source`, which tracks the source dictionary's numerical recipe.
- Finite restoration: `prunedUE_sparseEndpoint_cnExtendible_unbounded` proves unbounded extendibles in the selected endpoint itself. Despite its name, its endpoint theorem assumes UE, sufficient extendibility, failure of internal AC and a generic; it does not assume an externally countable ground or absence of rank-Berkeley cardinals. Downward closure gives all smaller positive levels in the same model.
- Class-forcing criterion: the formal theorem uses `ClassForcingDensitySemantics`, an explicit interface for precisely the forcing theorem, generic-through-condition, same-ordinals and required class-comprehension assumptions. Those are hypotheses in the paper's conditional proposition. This audit accepts this semantic encoding; it does not assert a separate two-sorted GBC formalization of that interface.
- Internal/external distinction: internal DC, ultrafilter completeness, coded embedding graphs and countable sequences are statements about sets in the models. Standard external indices are used for the recursive schemes, as in the manuscript. The nonzero rank-Berkeley restriction matches V13's definition.
